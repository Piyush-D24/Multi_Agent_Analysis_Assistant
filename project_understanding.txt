File-wise explanation:

1. agents.yaml:
Defines name, role, goal, backstory, tools used, and some other characteristics used for the 3 agents: supervisor agent, data scientist agent and data analyst agent.

Supervisor agent:
It acts as team lead and request router by delegating specific tasks to the other 2 agents.
It reads the user's request, looks at the chat history, classify the request into it's category.
It is told that it is a senior analytics team lead at a data driven company, and is skilled in managing analytics and scientists.
tools_used:
    - classify_user_request: classifies user request into pre-defined categories.
    - create_agent_work_plan: Generate a step by step work plan for the agents.
    - summarize_chat_history: Compress previous conversations into a short summary to minimize context window
    - validate_final_response_structure: Checks whether the response generated contains some pre-required sections.
    - estimate_context_usage: Make an estimation of how much context window is being used.

Data Analyst Agent:
It acts as a business data analyst and SQL expert.
It reviews datasets, suggests business performance indicators(KPIs), writes or double-checks safe SQL queries, designs dashboard layouts, and translates numbers into clear business insights.
It is told it has 10 years experience in ecommerce, fintech and SaaS analytics.
tools_used:
    - profile_dataframe: Reads a CSV and returns filename, row/column count, list of columns, column dtypes, nulls and missing values, duplicates and sample records.
